README.md · why this exists

Chaos doesn't take December off

Somewhere in the world, right now, a company is finding out it was breached. A password database is being leaked, a ransomware gang is setting a deadline, or a popular package is shipping something it shouldn't. This happens every single day, and most of it never reaches the news you read.

The Advent of Chaos is a small, festive way of making that visible. Instead of chocolate, each box holds one real incident from that day, so by Christmas you've seen 24 days of what is normally invisible.

Why it matters more in the age of AI

  • Attacks got cheaper. AI writes convincing phishing in any language, clones voices and helps find and weaponise bugs faster than ever.
  • The attack surface grew. Everyone is wiring AI assistants, agents and plugins into their apps and data, often faster than they secure them.
  • Trust is harder. When a message, a video or a call can be faked, "it looked real" stops being a defence.

Awareness, not fear

The point isn't to scare you. It's that people who know what attacks look like are much harder to fool. A few habits beat most of what you'll see in these boxes:

  • 🔑 Use a password manager, and passkeys or two-factor wherever you can.
  • ⏳ Treat urgency as a red flag. "Act now" is the oldest trick there is.
  • 🔄 Install updates. Many boxes here are bugs that already had a fix.
  • 💾 Keep backups you could actually restore from.
  • 🤖 Be careful what you paste into AI tools, and what you let them do on your behalf.

How the calendar works

Every couple of hours a bot reads the security news, and an AI picks the most notable incident of the day and writes a short summary with links to the original reporting. Once a box is filled it is frozen forever. Quiet days get a nerd joke, because even defenders need a break. 🎄

Stay curious, stay patched, and happy holidays.
