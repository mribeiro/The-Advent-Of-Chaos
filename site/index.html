The Advent of Chaos

24 days. 24 incidents. Zero chill.

Locked Elves scanning Incident Quiet day (joke)

Every day, the elves scan the security news. The first notable breach, hack or attack they find gets wrapped and frozen forever. If the day passes quietly (up to 12:00 UTC the day after), you get a nerd joke instead.

Summaries are written by an AI and link to the original reporting. All times are UTC.
